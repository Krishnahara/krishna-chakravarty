I collaborate on materials development, scale-up strategy, and proposal coordination — at the boundary between science and industry.

## What I can help with

- **Deep-tech commercialization & scale-up strategy** — moving a technology from bench through pilot to industrial demonstration (TRL 2–7), with a realistic read on the coupled barriers ahead.
- **Grant writing & consortium development** — EU / Horizon, Nordic, Danish and UN-linked instruments: technical narratives, work packages, and multi-partner consortium design.
- **Technical due diligence** — for investors in climate-tech and hard-to-abate sectors, drawing on first-hand experience of leading it from the venture side.
- **Certification & compliance navigation** — ISO 8217, ISCC EU, EBC / Puro.earth, RED II/III, and lifecycle assessment (ISO 14040/44).
- **Feedstock & precursor selection, thermochemical process design** — pyrolysis and gasification for fuels, biochar/CDR, and now hard-carbon anode materials.
- **Applied-AI workflow automation** — document and data harmonization, translation, and model-reliability engineering.
- **Teaching, supervision & executive education** — entrepreneurship and innovation, taught from lived venture cases.
