## Problem

Proving a technology in a European lab is one thing; deploying it in a developing-country context, with real feedstock and real logistics, is another. The UNIDO-backed project asked for a working demonstration in the Tanga region of Tanzania.

## Approach

Coordinated installation and demonstration of a 9 t/day cashew-waste processing system, running the delivery on an agile/SCRUM basis and integrating DTU Risø's two-stage gasifier design known for high biomass-to-electricity conversion.

## Result

A functioning 9 t/day demonstration delivered under a UNIDO contract (~2.8M DKK), part of a broader EU–Africa–India deployment corridor and a template for distributed, containerised plants.
