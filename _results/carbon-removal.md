## Problem

Carbon-dioxide removal only counts if it is measurable, permanent, and independently verified. Biochar — the solid carbon skeleton left by pyrolysis — can sequester carbon durably when applied to soil, but only within rigorous certification frameworks.

## Approach

Led the carbon-MRV and certification work: establishing biochar value chains under the European Biochar Certificate and ISCC, running CDR research on permanence, stability and lifecycle carbon accounting, and building traceable carbon accounting under ISO 14040/44 and GHG-Protocol-aligned reporting.

## Result

The programme supported issuance of roughly 8,000 tCO₂e of verified credits, alongside on the order of 3,000 tonnes of biochar and 1,500 tonnes of biofuel-equivalent output — with third-party verification (Boundless Impact) and work with the Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping.
