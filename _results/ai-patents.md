## Problem

Generative AI tools for grant writing often sacrifice scientific accuracy for fluency, producing plausible but unverifiable outputs.

## Approach

Co-developed agentic AI architectures with structured reasoning pipelines, validation layers, and feedback loops that preserve domain fidelity.

## Result

4 patents filed (WO/2025). Systems deployed for industrial R&D workflow automation at Fortune-100 logistics scale — reported at ~200 ms latency across 216 languages with substantial GPU-cost reduction.

### Patents

- WO/2025/141391 — AI system for randomized dataset creation and evaluation of emotional-state detection
- WO/2025/233926 — Hallucination-mitigation system for generative AI
- WO/2025/233925 — Multi-computer neural-architecture system for content generation
- US 18/927,834 — Neural-translation system with domain adaptation
